About

Some phones close background apps to save battery. When that happens, calls, messages and mail arrive late or not at all, and it is hard to tell which setting is to blame.

I built NEXGUARD after my own phone did exactly this. Every restart killed the apps running in the background, and I missed important calls, messages and emails. NEXGUARD does not fight the operating system. It records what Android delivers, checks what can be checked, and points you to the settings on your phone. Where Android will not say, it says so.

The developer

Syed Muhammad Hussain Shah, Pakistan. I plan to finish the app over the next few updates.

Where it is tested

OPPO CPH1909, ColorOS 5.2.1, Android 8.1.0. Other phones and Android versions are untested.

What it cannot do

It cannot stop other apps from being closed, restart force-stopped apps, or recover notifications that never reached your phone.
