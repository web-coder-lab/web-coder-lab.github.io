Find out why your notifications go quiet.

NEXGUARD is a free, open-source Android app. It records the notifications Android delivers, checks the settings that block background apps, and shows you the fix for your phone.

Download for AndroidTry it in your browser
NEXGUARD 1.0.3Syed Muhammad Hussain ShahFree, open source, 13.5 MB

Record what Android actually delivered.

Every notification that is posted or removed, for the apps you choose. Saved on your phone with the app, the time and the event type. No message text.

Check what can be checked.

Notification access, monitoring state and battery policy, as Android reports them. Where Android will not say, NEXGUARD does not guess.

Then fix it, for your phone.

OPPO, Samsung, Xiaomi, Vivo, Huawei, OnePlus and others close background apps. NEXGUARD points you to the exact settings. The OPPO guide matches the developer's own phone.

Phone guides

Why NEXGUARD exists

Built because a reboot and an aggressive battery manager can silence calls and messages. The app records the notification events you choose, checks the settings you can control, and stays on the phone. There is no account and no custom analytics server.

This site is a normal GitHub Pages site. HTTPS hides the page path from the network path in many cases, but GitHub still serves the page. It is not Tor, and it is not safer than Tor. Uninstalling the app removes its private files. Flash storage can still leave old blocks. NEXGUARD does not claim forensic erasure.

The other site is NOT BYPASS, a private Android browser.
