About this app

NEXGUARD records when notifications reach your phone, checks the settings that can block background apps, and shows phone-brand guidance for the settings you can change. History and preferences stay on your device.

It cannot stop Android or a phone maker from closing other apps, restart a force-stopped app, or recover a notification that never reached the phone.

What's new

Version 1.0.3, 20 October 2026

  • Settings footer shows NEXGUARD and the real version number.
  • The English interface uses monitoring wording instead of protection wording.
  • 25 language options. All text is translated, but the translations are machine-generated and not yet reviewed by native speakers.
  • New installs monitor user-installed apps by default; system apps start excluded. Existing choices are not overwritten.
  • Backup and device-transfer rules exclude the notification database and preferences.
  • An About section shows the version, source link and license.
  • The unused FOREGROUND_SERVICE permission was removed. The app does not start a foreground service.

Data safety

  • No data shared with third partiesThe app does not request the internet permission.
  • No data collected by the developerNo accounts, analytics or crash-reporting service.
  • Data stays on your deviceEvent history and preferences are stored locally. Backup is switched off.
  • You can delete your dataUse Clear Event Log in Settings, or uninstall the app.

This is the developer's statement. It matches the permissions in the APK. It has not been independently audited.

Permissions

POST_NOTIFICATIONSShows the app's own diagnostic notices on Android 13 and later.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETEDReschedules diagnostics after the phone restarts.
REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONSOpens the system screen where you can exempt NEXGUARD from battery limits.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATEReads whether a network is available for the diagnostics screen. It is not used to upload data.
VIBRATESupports local alert feedback.
QUERY_ALL_PACKAGESLists installed apps so you can choose which ones to monitor. This is broad app visibility.
WAKE_LOCKUsed by the background-work library.
Notification access (system setting)Required by Android so the app can observe posted and removed notifications.
