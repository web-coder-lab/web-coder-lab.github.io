How this was made

Nothing here is a screen recording. The screens are rebuilt in HTML, CSS and SVG from the app's source code: the four theme palettes from Color.kt, the shield's curve and its 2.2 second pulse from GuardianShield.kt, the layout sizes from the Compose screens, and every word from the app's own string table.

The tour taps the same buttons you can tap. Sounds are generated in your browser and stay off until you switch them on. The demo shows what the app looks like, not live data from your phone.
